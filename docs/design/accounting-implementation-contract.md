# Accounting implementation contract

Latest user policy, 2026-09-12: earned income comes only from the 4% welfare deduction. General accounting credit is not synonymous with income: advances credit liabilities. Fees/dues from reference templates are disabled/out of active scope; their annual report can explicitly show not applicable under this policy.

## Architecture

Reuse AppDbContext and local SQLite, split accounting configuration into a partial AppDbContext file. Append-only journal headers and lines; lines include optional member, death and beneficiary dimensions. Latest clarification: maintain TWO SEPARATE BOOKS, `welfare` (member prepaid/welfare) and `association` (operating association). Every account/journal belongs to a book; statements and bank balances are separated by book. A single journal cannot cross books. Book-specific cash/bank assignments. One earned-income account, only in association: 4000 (4% welfare deduction). Other standard accounts: 1000 cash, configurable bank accounts; welfare 2000 member advance liability, 2100 welfare payable, 1200 member contribution shortfall receivable, 2200 fee due to association; association 1300 fee due from welfare; 3000 opening accumulated fund; association 5000+ categorized operating expenses. Opening equity is explicit supported opening balance, never an unexplained balancing income entry.

Fee accrual is a linked atomic pair of balanced postings: welfare debits funded member advances / shortfall receivable, credits net benefit payable and fee due to association; association debits fee due from welfare and credits 4% fee income. Later actual fee remittance is another linked pair: welfare debits due-to and credits its bank; association debits its bank and credits due-from. It does NOT recognize income again. Do not automatically consolidate the books or treat member welfare balances as unrestricted operating money. Separate-book trial balances and statements are the default. If a combined overview is added it must eliminate reciprocal due-to/due-from balances and label the basis.

Every posting has business date, recorded UTC, number, type, description, actor, unique request token and request fingerprint. Replay an identical request safely; reject token reuse with changed payload. Persist posting + lines + business effects + audit atomically. Check nonzero positive amounts, exact satang, DateOnly bounds, balanced entries, account roles, member/beneficiary references and closed periods. Preserve source snapshots and document identity. A posted reversal references its original and cannot be repeated; refuse reversals whose downstream settlements would make balances inconsistent.

Opening is explicit: date and cash/bank/member-advance/unpaid-benefit balances backed by evidence; no automatic accounting activation or fabricated receipts when existing demo data is present. Once activated, existing counter reset cannot create paid advance. Existing unpaid contributor units may be negative: surface a receivable instead of inventing funded advances. Freeze monetary movements and rates; later settings do not revalue posted receipts.

Death benefit calculation remains the current domain authority. Accrual must use its immutable snapshot, distinguish 4% fee income, net welfare payable and return of deceased advance. Do not pay on accrual. Existing/pre-cutover cases require an explicit supported opening amount; do not post historical full benefits automatically. Post payouts per beneficiary against their entitlement. Zero/nonpay cases must not generate income or positive welfare liability. Inactive accounting leaves the existing first-day workflow intact.

## Reporting contract

Reports derive from posted journals and their dimensions, include reversals exactly once, use business-date cutoffs and show opening/movements/closing. Trial balance checks debit/credit; statement of position checks assets = liabilities + accumulated funds + current result; income/expense includes only account categories income/expense. Cash movement reports show internal transfers distinctly. Individual advance totals must agree to the control account, unpaid benefit totals to welfare payable. Printed reissues retain original document data and status; reminders are printable, never auto-sent, and do not auto-terminate membership.

The UI offers task-oriented pages for setup/opening, receipts/group receipt, collection requests, member ledger, benefit accrual/payout, expenses/transfers/adjustments, voucher history/reversal, closing/reconciliation and reports. Follow existing Thai date validation, antiforgery forms, decimal-to-satang validation and download patterns. Explain actionable business facts rather than technical implementation details.
